02. The renaming
AY Studio ———> Astutely.
 Same hand · sharper pencil 

After nine years as AY Studio we have changed our name — not the team, not the people, not the work. The new name reflects how we already think: carefully, clearly and with intent. Astute. Considered. Clear-eyed about what work needs to do.

03. The proposition

Most brands do not need more marketing. They need better decisions.

01 / Clarity first Get the brief right before anything moves.

Every project goes through a clarity check — positioning, audience and message agreed before execution.

02 / Diagnosis Understand the business before recommending anything.

We don’t template our thinking. Diagnosis before prescription, every time.

03 / Built to run Senior team, hands-on, designed to last.

The people you meet are the people who do the work. Everything is structured for your team to own.

04 / Calm authority Considered counsel without manufactured urgency.

Quiet iteration over loud reinventions. Useful work, on schedule, in plain language.
