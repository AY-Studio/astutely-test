Build a luxury, slick, single-page website for “Astutely”, a Creative Marketing Agency, formerly AY Studio.

The site should feel premium enough to be shared. It must not feel like a standard agency website. It should feel like a confident editorial brand launch page with a clever central idea:

CORE HOOK:
“Sharp thinking, beautifully applied.”

Use this as the creative thread throughout the site. The website should position Astutely as the agency for businesses that do not need more marketing noise — they need sharper judgement, clearer direction and better execution.

STYLE:
- Luxury editorial design
- Minimal but memorable
- Black / warm white / soft stone palette
- Elegant typography
- Large cinematic spacing
- Slick transitions
- Subtle scroll animations
- Premium, calm, confident tone
- No generic icons
- No loud colours
- No stock imagery
- Use tasteful abstract visual details: fine lines, oversized punctuation, subtle word reveals, editorial numbering, thin borders, soft motion

CLEVER WEBSITE IDEA:
Create a recurring visual motif around “sharpness”.
Examples:
- Thin precision lines
- Copy that “cuts through” the page
- Section transitions that reveal copy like a blade edge
- A small recurring phrase: “Cut through the noise.”
- Use editorial pull quotes to make the page feel ownable and shareable

STRUCTURE AND EXACT COPY:

Large headline:
Sharp thinking, beautifully applied.

Subheading:
Astutely is a creative marketing agency for brands that need clearer direction, stronger ideas and work that feels as considered as it is effective.

Make the hero feel cinematic and premium. Use lots of space. Add a subtle animated line or word reveal around the word “Sharp”.

The shift

Heading:
Not a rebrand for the sake of it.

Astutely is built for business owners, founders and teams who want marketing to feel less complicated and more effective.

Cut through the noise.

3. MANIFESTO SECTION

Section label:

Heading:
Most brands do not need more marketing. They need better decisions.

We help brands pause, sharpen the message and move with purpose. Then we turn that clarity into brand, marketing, PR and digital work that people actually notice.

Design note:
This section should feel highly shareable. Treat the heading like a bold statement poster.

--------------------------------------------------

INTERACTION DETAILS:
- Smooth scroll
- Subtle fade and reveal animations
- Hero text should animate in slowly and confidently
- Use fine-line dividers between sections
- Use hover states that feel precise, not playful
- Cards can subtly lift or reveal a thin line on hover
- Keep performance lightweight

IMPORTANT RULES:
- Do not add testimonials
- Do not add fake case studies
- Do not use stock photos
- Do not rewrite the provided copy
- Do not make it colourful or playful
- Do not make it look like a SaaS landing page
- The final result should feel luxury, intelligent, restrained and memorable
